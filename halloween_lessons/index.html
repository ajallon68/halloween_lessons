The mini lesson

Meet the suffix -less

Add -less to a noun to make an adjective meaning without something or not having it. In Portuguese, it often means “sem”.

hope → hopelesscare → careless

A tiny spelling tip: usually, keep the original noun and add -less. Then use the new adjective to describe someone or something: a cloudless sky, a fearless cat.

Horror movie vocabulary

What kind of scary story do you like?

Thriller
A suspenseful story full of tension and surprises.
Slasher / gory
A killer, chase scenes, and often bloody, graphic violence.
Ghost story
A story about spirits haunting the living.
Poltergeist
A noisy spirit said to move objects; often part of supernatural horror.
Psychological horror
Fear built around the mind, paranoia, and uncertainty.
Monster horror
A frightening creature threatens the characters.

Scary describes how a movie feels. Gory describes graphic blood and violence.

Halloween character files

Meet the monsters & mischief-makers

Read each short profile, learn a curious detail, and add a few new words to your spooky-story vocabulary.

Brazilian folklore

Saci

A mischievous, one-legged trickster who travels in a whirlwind and wears a red cap.

Curiosity: Folktales say his cap gives him magical powers; catching him may mean taking the cap.

Nightmare villain

Freddy Krueger

A horror villain who haunts people in their dreams, wearing a striped sweater and a bladed glove.

Curiosity: His glove turns an ordinary nightmare into a very recognizable movie prop.

Vampire legend

Dracula

A mysterious vampire, usually shown with a cape, sharp fangs, and a taste for blood.

Curiosity: Bram Stoker’s novel Dracula was published in 1897.

Classic creature

Frankenstein

A scientist creates a living being from body parts; people often call the creature Frankenstein, too.

Curiosity: Mary Shelley’s novel was first published in 1818.

Undead horror

Zombies

Reanimated dead characters often appear in scary stories about survival and the end of the world.

Curiosity: Modern zombie stories draw on Haitian folklore and later horror films.

Ancient mystery

Mummy

A preserved body wrapped in bandages, often brought back to life in supernatural tales.

Curiosity: Mummification was an ancient burial practice; the mummy’s curse is a later horror legend.

Gothic comedy

Wednesday Addams

A deadpan, clever member of the Addams family who finds the strange side of life fascinating.

Curiosity: Her name connects to the rhyme “Wednesday’s child is full of woe.”

Spooky symbol

Skeleton

A skeleton is the framework of bones inside a body and a classic Halloween costume.

Curiosity: Most adults have 206 bones.

Shapeshifter

Werewolf

A person who transforms into a wolf-like creature, often when the full moon appears.

Curiosity: Werewolf legends appear in stories from many different cultures.

Masked slasher

Ghostface

A masked figure from the Scream films who calls and frightens victims.

Curiosity: The Ghostface identity is taken up by different people across the films.
